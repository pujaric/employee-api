1️⃣ Code Quality and Standards

Follow clean code principles and maintain readable, modular, and maintainable code.

Ensure adherence to organization coding standards and best practices.

Reduce SonarQube code smells, vulnerabilities, and technical debt.

Increase unit test coverage and maintain minimum agreed coverage percentage.

Perform effective peer code reviews and incorporate feedback proactively.

Ensure proper logging, exception handling, and performance optimization.

2️⃣ Developer Excellence (Manifesto Alignment)

Demonstrate ownership and accountability for assigned deliverables.

Continuously improve technical skills through learning and certifications.

Actively participate in design discussions and provide constructive inputs.

Support team collaboration and knowledge sharing.

Follow Agile principles and contribute to sprint planning and retrospectives.

Focus on delivering business value with quality and efficiency.

Promote innovation and suggest automation or process improvements.

3️⃣ Jira and SDLC Compliance

Ensure accurate and timely updates of Jira tickets (status, comments, effort).

Maintain proper documentation for requirements, design, and implementation.

Follow SDLC processes strictly (design review, code review, testing, release).

Ensure all deliverables meet Definition of Done criteria.

Maintain traceability between requirements, development, and testing.

Participate in release planning and ensure deployment readiness.
