✅ Revised Goals for 12 Years Java Experience
1️⃣ Code Quality and Engineering Standards (Senior Ownership)

Define and enforce coding standards across the team.

Drive reduction of technical debt and improve Sonar quality gate compliance to 0 critical issues.

Review and approve critical code changes and architectural decisions.

Improve application performance, scalability, and security.

Introduce best practices (design patterns, clean architecture, modularization).

Ensure minimum 80–90% unit test coverage for critical modules.

Lead root cause analysis for production issues and implement preventive measures.

2️⃣ Developer Excellence (Manifesto Alignment – Leadership Level)

Act as technical mentor for junior and mid-level developers.

Take end-to-end ownership of features from design to production.

Drive design discussions and provide scalable, future-ready solutions.

Promote automation (CI/CD improvements, test automation, build optimization).

Lead knowledge sharing sessions within the team.

Align development activities with business objectives and delivery timelines.

Encourage innovation and continuous improvement culture.

3️⃣ Jira and SDLC Compliance (Process Champion)

Ensure 100% accurate estimation and sprint commitment reliability.

Enforce Definition of Ready and Definition of Done.

Improve sprint predictability and reduce spillovers.

Maintain high-quality documentation (design docs, API contracts).

Ensure proper change management and release governance compliance.

Support audit readiness and compliance requirements.

Identify SDLC process gaps and propose improvements.
